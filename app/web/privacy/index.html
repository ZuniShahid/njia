Privacy policy

Njia Careers (web, Android, iOS) · Last updated: 5 November 2026

Njia helps you plan a path to study or work in Europe. To do that it needs some information about you. This page explains what we store, why, who else processes it, and what you can do about it.

2. What we store and why

DataWhy
Account: e-mail address, name, password (stored only as a hash by Google Firebase Authentication). If you sign in with Google: your Google account name and e-mail.To give you an account and keep your plan safe.
Profile: nationality, country and city of residence, phone number (optional), birth year, education (schools, fields, grades, years), languages and test scores, work experience, interests and strengths, passport validity date, preferred countries, earliest move year.To compare your profile with the requirements of each study or work route and show your gaps.

Legal bases (EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR): providing the service you signed up for (Art. 6(1)(b)); your consent for optional data such as outcome sharing and any health or religious information you choose to enter (Art. 6(1)(a), Art. 9(2)(a)); our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure (Art. 6(1)(f)).

We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use analytics or advertising trackers in the app. Job rankings are never for sale.

3. Who processes your data for us

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC): sign-in, database, file storage, server functions and website hosting. Your data is stored in Google data centres in Europe (Firestore multi-region "eur3", Storage and functions in Belgium, europe-west1). Google processes the data on our behalf under its data processing terms; support or security access from outside the EU can happen under those terms.
  • Google Sign-In, only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Anthropic (Anthropic PBC, USA), only when you use the Coach: your question, the chat history of that session, your goal and plan, and the profile details the plan uses (nationality, country of residence, birth year, education, languages, work experience and the things the plan must respect) are sent to Anthropic's Claude model to write the answer. Your e-mail, phone number and documents are not sent. Anthropic processes this on our behalf under its data processing addendum and its commercial terms say it may not train its models on this content. Do not put information into the Coach that you do not want processed this way.

The country facts, requirements and job postings you see come from public official sources and job APIs (e.g. Adzuna). These sources do not receive your personal data. If you open a job posting, you leave Njia and the employer's or job site's own privacy policy applies.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, goals, plans, progress, outcomes and uploaded documents are deleted from our database and file storage, and your login is removed. Copies in Google's backup and log systems are erased by Google on its own schedule.

5. Your rights

  • See and correct your data at any time in the app (Profile).
  • Download a copy: Profile → Privacy & data → Export my data.
  • Delete your account and all its data: Profile → Privacy & data → Delete account. Without the app: njia.keevana.com/delete-account.
  • Withdraw a consent at any time (for example switch off outcome sharing or delete information you entered).
  • Object to processing, ask for restriction, or ask for your data in a portable format: write to us at the address in section 1.
  • Complain to a data protection authority. Ours is the Commission for Personal Data Protection, Bulgaria (cpdp.bg). You can also contact the authority where you live.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google Firebase. Access rules make sure only you can read your uploaded documents and plans. Your profile can also be read by a small number of Njia administrators, only to help you or to fix problems. Content editors see change requests about public country information, not your personal data.

7. Age

Njia is meant for people aged 16 and older. If you are younger, please do not create an account.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. For important changes we tell you in the app.
