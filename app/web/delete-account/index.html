Delete your Njia account

Njia Careers · operated by Revana EOOD, Bulgaria

In the app (fastest)

  1. Sign in to Njia Careers (app or njia.keevana.com).
  2. Open Profile → Privacy & data.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted right away.

Without the app

Send an e-mail from the address you use for Njia to info@keevana.com with the subject "Delete my Njia account". We delete your account within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted

  • Your login (e-mail or Google sign-in).
  • Your profile, goals, plans, progress and reported outcomes.
  • All documents you uploaded.

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you. Copies in Google's backup and log systems are erased by Google on its own schedule.
